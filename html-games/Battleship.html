Easy: fires at random. Medium: hunts on a checkerboard, then chases hits along a line. Hard: calculates a probability map of every place the remaining ships could fit. Impossible: the robot checks every fleet layout that fits what it has seen (up to 10 seconds per shot) and fires at the likeliest square. It cannot see your ships, so a lucky player can still win. Choose who shoots first with Goes first. Hints: a heat map or checkerboard shows good squares to fire at. When a game ends you can save it as JSON, load a saved game and replay it, or use Instant replay for the latest match. Two players: both place ships and take turns on one screen, with a hand-over screen between turns. Power-ups (flip the ⚡ switch before the battle): each player gets 2 super missiles, 1 radar, 2 hidden shields (placed during ship setup, up to 2 on one square), 2 bombs, 3 undos (take back your last move and the reply, free), and unlimited flags and shovel use. The robot gets none.
