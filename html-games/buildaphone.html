Build Your Dream Phone

Pick parts from the bin and put them on your phone: screens, ports, lenses, rotors, rockets, toasters. A phone needs a screen, a battery and a CPU (any chip, even a snail) to power on. Its OS depends on your CPU, RAM and storage, so a trash CPU with 1 MB of RAM runs a very different phone than a super chip with 16 GB.
FRONT

Your phone

Desktop: drag a part onto the phone, drag placed parts to move them, and drag one off the stage to remove it. Mobile: tap a part to add it, then drag it into place. Select a placed part to rotate, resize, copy or remove it (keys: R, + , −, Delete). Use Flip phone to build the back too (for example a telescope on the front and another on the back). Then press Power on and use your phone: the apps depend on the parts you built.
