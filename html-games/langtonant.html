Classic rule: on a blank square turn right, on a coloured square turn left. After about 10,000 steps a diagonal highway appears.

Each step: the ant looks at the colour of its square, turns (R = right, L = left, N = straight, U = turn around) according to the rule, changes that square to the next colour, then moves forward. A rule with more letters means more colours. Click the grid to add ants and watch them interact.
