Game of Life

Draw cells and watch them live, die and multiply. Press Space to play or pause.

Rules: a live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours survives, a dead cell with exactly 3 live neighbours is born, everything else dies or stays dead. Other rules use the same idea with different numbers (B = born, S = survive). Draw by dragging, or choose Stamp to drop a pattern such as a glider or the Gosper glider gun.
